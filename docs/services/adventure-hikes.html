Questions about adventure hikes

Are dogs off leash on adventure hikes?

No. Every hike is on leash for safety and to follow local trail rules. A long line may be used in appropriate areas with your approval.

Where do you hike?

Common choices include the Ridgeline Trail system, Pre’s Trail and the paths around Alton Baker Park, chosen based on weather and your dog’s fitness.

What happens on very hot or smoky days?

We shorten the outing, move to shaded routes, or reschedule. Safety comes first in heat and poor air quality.
