Questions about drop-in visits

How many drop-in visits does my dog need per day?

Most adult dogs do well with two to three visits a day when you are away. Puppies and seniors may need more frequent breaks.

Can you give medication?

Yes. We give oral medications and follow written instructions from you or your vet. Please leave meds clearly labeled.

Do you take care of cats during drop-ins?

Yes. Cats and other household pets are cared for during the same visit at no fuss.
