Questions about overnight pet sitting

What hours does an overnight sitter stay?

Overnight stays typically run from early evening to the next morning. Midday care can be added with a walk or drop-in visit.

Is overnight sitting better than boarding?

For many dogs, yes. Staying home keeps routines, smells and sleep the same, which lowers stress and avoids kennel exposure.

How far ahead should I book overnight care?

Two to four weeks is ideal. For holidays, UO graduation and big Eugene event weekends, book as early as possible.
