Pet Taxi FAQ for Willakenzie

Do you offer pet taxi in Willakenzie (97401)?

Yes. Pet Taxi is available throughout Willakenzie and nearby Ferry Street Bridge and Cal Young.

Where do you drive with dogs in Willakenzie?

From Willakenzie, most rides reach vets, groomers and daycares across Eugene in about 10 to 20 minutes. Busier arterials are crossed at signals and avoided when possible.

Is my dog secured during the ride?

Always. Dogs ride in a crate or a crash-tested style harness clipped to a seat belt. Loose dogs are never transported.

Can you wait during a vet appointment?

Yes. We can stay for the appointment, relay notes from the vet, or drop off and return for pickup.
