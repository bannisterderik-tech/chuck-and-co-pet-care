Pet Taxi FAQ for Southeast Neighbors

Do you offer pet taxi in Southeast Neighbors (97405)?

Yes. Pet Taxi is available throughout Southeast Neighbors and nearby South Eugene and Fairmount.

Where do you drive with dogs in Southeast Neighbors?

From Southeast Neighbors, most rides reach vets, groomers and daycares across Eugene in about 10 to 20 minutes. Hillier blocks to the south give energetic dogs extra challenge.

Is my dog secured during the ride?

Always. Dogs ride in a crate or a crash-tested style harness clipped to a seat belt. Loose dogs are never transported.

Can you wait during a vet appointment?

Yes. We can stay for the appointment, relay notes from the vet, or drop off and return for pickup.
