Foxtails and Grass Seeds: A Summer Hazard for Oregon Dogs

What foxtails are, why they are dangerous for dogs, and how to check after walks.

Short answer

Foxtails are barbed grass seeds common in Oregon summers that can work into a dog’s paws, ears, nose and eyes. Avoid dry, tall grass, check your dog after every walk and see a vet for sudden head shaking, sneezing, limping or squinting.

Where you’ll find them

Dry fields, trail edges and vacant lots from late spring through summer.

The after-walk check

  • Between toes and paw pads
  • Inside and around ears
  • Armpits, groin and under the collar

When to call the vet

Persistent sneezing, head shaking, licking one paw or a swollen spot can mean a seed has embedded.

FAQ

Do your walkers check for foxtails?

Yes, during foxtail season every walk ends with a quick check.

Does trimming fur help?

A summer trim around paws and ears can make seeds easier to spot.
