Booking Holiday Pet Sitting in Eugene

When to book pet sitting for holidays and busy Eugene event weekends.

Short answer

Book holiday pet sitting in Eugene three to six weeks ahead for Thanksgiving, winter holidays, spring break, UO graduation and major event weekends. Availability fills quickly, and early booking guarantees your preferred sitter.

Busy times

  • Thanksgiving and late December
  • Spring break
  • UO commencement weekend
  • Summer travel and major event weekends

What to plan

Share travel dates, flight times and any changes as soon as you know them.

Peace of mind

Your dog stays home and keeps their routine while you enjoy the trip.

FAQ

How far ahead should I book?

Three to six weeks for holidays. Sooner is better.

Do you work on holidays?

Yes, holiday care is available.
