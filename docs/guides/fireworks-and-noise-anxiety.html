Fireworks, Game Days and Noise Anxiety

How to help your dog through Fourth of July fireworks and noisy Eugene event days.

Short answer

Help your dog through fireworks and loud events by walking earlier in the day, closing windows and curtains, playing background noise, offering a safe hiding spot and making sure ID tags and microchip info are current.

Before the noise

Walk early and use a secure harness. Escapes spike around fireworks.

During the noise

  • A quiet interior room or covered crate
  • White noise or music
  • Chews or food puzzles

Talk to your vet

If your dog panics, your vet can discuss options ahead of time.

FAQ

Can you sit with my dog on the Fourth?

Yes, holiday overnight and evening visits are available. Book early.

Do you walk during fireworks?

No, we schedule walks well before dark on fireworks nights.

General information for Eugene dog owners, not veterinary or legal advice. Always check with your vet and official sources.
